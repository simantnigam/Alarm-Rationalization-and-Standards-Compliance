# NorthPlant Site Operating Policy

## 3 Alarm Rationalization Supplement

### 3.1 Applicability

This section supplements, and does not replace, `ALM-CRIT-003`. Where this policy
states a numeric threshold, that threshold applies at NorthPlant in place of the
corresponding value in `ALM-CRIT-003`; where it is silent, `ALM-CRIT-003` governs
directly.

### 3.2 Occurrence Evidence at NorthPlant

Consistent with `ALM-CRIT-003` §3.1, an alarm at NorthPlant shall not be considered for
suppression or shelving unless it has occurred **25 or more times within the preceding
90 days**. NorthPlant operations has reviewed and adopted the corporate threshold
without modification.
